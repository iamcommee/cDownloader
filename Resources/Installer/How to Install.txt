How to install cDownloader

1. Drag cDownloader onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open cDownloader from your Applications folder.

Everything for YouTube and video downloads is included. There is nothing else to install.


The first time you open it

macOS may say it could not check cDownloader for harmful software. It says this because cDownloader is
not registered with Apple, not because something is wrong. You only need to do this once:

  a. Click "Done" (not "Move to Trash").
  b. Open System Settings and choose Privacy & Security.
  c. Scroll down to the message about cDownloader and click "Open Anyway".
  d. Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click "Open Anyway" once more.

From then on, cDownloader opens normally.


Using it with your browser

To let cDownloader take over downloads from Chrome, Edge or Brave, open cDownloader's Settings,
choose Browser, and follow the three steps shown there.
